Privacy Policy

Adhan App • Last updated: September 21, 2026

At Adhan App, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding your data when you use our mobile application.

1. No Personal Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information. You do not need to create an account, provide an email address, or input any personal details to use this application.

2. Local On-Device Storage

All data, preferences, calculation settings, or saved prayer logs generated within the app are processed and stored exclusively on your device.

  • We do not operate external servers to collect or store your user data.
  • If you delete the app from your device, all data associated with the app will be permanently removed.

3. Analytics and Tracking

  • We do not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising trackers, or telemetry tools to monitor your behavior.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

4. Third-Party Services

The app operates completely independently and does not transmit your information to third-party services, advertisers, or data brokers.

5. App Permissions

If the app requests system permissions (such as Location for Qibla calculation and local prayer times, or Notifications for Adhan alerts), these permissions are used strictly on-device to enable specific feature functionality. No location or notification data ever leaves your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Because our application does not collect any personal information, it fully complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) standards regarding children's privacy.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
